# **Pre-Kidney Transplant Evaluation and Post-Transplant Monitoring Guide**

# **Part 1: Pre-KT Work-Up Process by Phase**

## **Phase I: Pre-Donation & Pre-Transplant Preparation**

* **Orientation & Screening:** Pre-transplant orientation for both recipient and potential donor candidates.  
* **Initial Assessment:** Initial clinical work-up by a Transplant Nephrologist/Fellow to identify medical contraindications.  
* **Work-Up Clearances & Evaluations:**  
  * Complete laboratory testing and diagnostic imaging for both recipient and donor.  
  * Multi-specialty medical clearances (Cardiology, Dental, Pulmonology, Endocrinology, Infectious Disease, Neuropsychiatry, Donor Advocate).  
  * Hospital Transplant Ethics Committee (HTEC) evaluation and approval.  
  * Case Discussion and Transplant Evaluation (CDTE) and risk stratification (Standard/Low Risk vs. High Risk).  
  * PhilHealth Z Package qualification and application.

# **Part 2: Detailed Pre-KT Laboratories & Diagnostics per Phase**

## **1\. Phase 1 Work-Up (Basic & Initial Screening)**

* **Hematology & Coagulation:**  
  * Complete Blood Count (CBC) with Differential  
  * Blood Typing (ABO and Rh)  
  * Bleeding Time, Clotting Time, Prothrombin Time (PT/INR), Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT)  
* **Blood Chemistry & Organ Function:**  
  * Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), HbA1c  
  * Kidney Function: Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN), Serum Uric Acid (SUA)  
  * Liver Function: SGPT/ALT, SGOT/AST, Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)  
  * Electrolytes & Minerals: Sodium (Na), Potassium (K), Total/Ionized Calcium (Ca), Phosphorus, Magnesium (Mg)  
  * Lipid Profile (Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, VLDL)  
  * Serum Albumin & Total Protein  
  * Intact Parathyroid Hormone (iPTH)  
* **Microbiology, Infectious Screening & Serology:**  
  * Hepatitis B Markers: HBsAg, Anti-HBs, Anti-HBc (Total/IgG/IgM), Hep B DNA (if indicated)  
  * Hepatitis C Screening: Anti-HCV  
  * Syphilis Screening: TPPA / VDRL / RPR  
  * HIV Screening & Malaria Screening (BSMP)  
  * Viral Titers: Cytomegalovirus (CMV IgG), Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV IgG), Varicella IgG  
  * Tuberculosis Screening: TB Quantiferon / Chest Radiograph  
  * Throat Swab (Gram Stain & Culture/Sensitivity)  
* **Urinalysis & Clinical Microscopy:**  
  * Routine Urinalysis & Urine Microscopy  
  * Urine Culture and Sensitivity  
  * Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (UACR) / 24-hour Urine Protein & Creatinine  
  * Fecalysis with Stool Occult Blood / FIT  
  * Serum / Urine Pregnancy Test (for females)  
* **Baseline Imaging & Diagnostic Work-Up:**  
  * Chest X-ray (PA view)  
  * 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG)  
  * Ultrasound of Whole Abdomen (Renal Ultrasound)  
  * 2D Echocardiogram with Doppler

## **2\. Phase 2 Work-Up (Immunologic & Specialized Diagnostics)**

* **Immunologic & Tissue Crossmatching Tests:**  
  * HLA Tissue Typing (Class I: A, B, C; Class II: DR, DQ, DP) for donor-recipient matching  
  * Panel Reactive Antibody (PRA) Screening (Class I, Class II, MICA)  
  * Single Antigen Bead (SAB) testing to check for Donor-Specific Antibodies (DSA)  
  * T-Cell & B-Cell Tissue Crossmatch (CDC / Flow Cytometry)  
* **Specialized Vascular & Anatomic Studies for Donor:**  
  * Renal CT Angiography with 3D Reconstruction  
  * Nuclear GFR Scan (Split Renal Function)  
  * Aorto-Iliac Duplex Ultrasound (Arteries & Veins)

## **3\. Phase 3 Work-Up (Pre-Admission & Immediate Pre-Operative)**

* **Pre-Admission Clearance Tests:**  
  * Repeat Chest X-ray (1 day prior to admission)  
  * Repeat Urinalysis & CBC  
  * RT-PCR Testing (done on the day of admission)  
  * Pre-transplant Hemodialysis / Peritoneal Dialysis session (if indicated for fluid/electrolyte optimization)

# **Part 3: Pre-KT Medical Clearances**

* **Cardiology Clearance:**  
  * Assessment of cardiovascular status, exercise tolerance, and operative risk.  
  * Diagnostics: 12-Lead ECG, 2D Echocardiogram with Doppler, and Dobutamine Stress Echocardiogram or Coronary Angiogram if high risk.  
* **Infectious Disease Clearance:**  
  * Screening for active, latent, or occult infections.  
  * Diagnostics: Review of viral markers (Hepatitis B/C, HIV, CMV, EBV, VDRL/TPPA), TB screening (TB-IGRA/Quantiferon or Chest X-ray), and vaccines/immunization history.  
* **Dental Clearance:**  
  * Dental examination and prophylaxis/extraction to eliminate potential oral foci of infection prior to immunosuppression.  
* **Neuropsychiatric Clearance:**  
  * Psychosocial evaluation of both recipient and donor candidates to evaluate cognitive capacity, mental stability, coping mechanisms, and support systems.  
  * Key Criteria: Confirms voluntary donation (no coercion/monetary compensation for donors) and assesses candidate adherence to post-transplant therapy.  
* **Gastroenterology / Hepatology Clearance (As Indicated):**  
  * Mandatory if HBsAg (+), Anti-HCV (+), or if abnormal liver enzymes / signs of chronic liver disease are present.  
  * Criteria: Confirmation of no liver cirrhosis and viral load checks (HBV DNA / HCV RNA).  
* **Pulmonology Clearance (As Indicated):**  
  * Candidates with a history of chronic lung disease, heavy smoking (\>= 30 pack-years), or abnormal Chest X-ray/CT results.  
* **Urology Clearance (As Indicated):**  
  * All candidates aged 50 years and above, or those with lower urinary tract symptoms, structural abnormalities, or prostate enlargement (PSA screening for males \> 50).  
* **Obstetrics & Gynecology Clearance (For Female Candidates):**  
  * Routine health screening including Pap Smear (for females \> 20\) and Mammogram (for females \> 40, or \> 35 if with family history).

# **Part 4: Post-Transplant Laboratory Monitoring Schedule**

## **A. Immediate Post-Operative Period (First Week / Inpatient)**

* **CBC with Differential:** Daily (Days 0 to 6\)  
* **Serum Creatinine & BUN:** Daily (Days 0 to 5\)  
* **Electrolytes (Potassium, Sodium):** Daily (Days 0 to 6\)  
* **Calcium:** Days 0 to 2  
* **Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS):** Days 0 and 1  
* **Liver Function Tests (SGPT, ALP):** Days 0 and 1  
* **Urinalysis:** Day 0 (and as indicated)  
* **Therapeutic Drug Level Monitoring (TDM \- Tacrolimus/Cyclosporine/Everolimus):** Days 0, 1, and 2 (and prior to dose adjustments)  
* **Renal Ultrasound / Graft Scan:** Done immediately post-op if clinically indicated (e.g., poor urine output or sudden creatinine spike).

## **B. Outpatient Follow-up Monitoring**

| Timeline | Frequency | Required Post-Transplant Laboratories |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1st Month** | **Weekly** | CBC, Serum Creatinine, BUN, FBS, Electrolytes (Na, K), ALT/SGPT, Urinalysis, Immunosuppressive Drug Trough Levels (Tacrolimus/Cyclosporine) |
| **2nd Month** | **Every 2 Weeks** | CBC, Serum Creatinine, BUN, FBS, Electrolytes, ALT/SGPT, Urinalysis, Immunosuppressive Drug Trough Levels |
| **3rd to 12th Month** | **Monthly** | CBC, Serum Creatinine, BUN, FBS, Electrolytes, ALT/SGPT, Urinalysis, Immunosuppressive Drug Trough Levels *(Include Lipid Profile monthly)* |
| **Beyond 1 Year** | **Every 1 to 3 Months** | Routine Graft Function Panel (CBC, Creatinine, Electrolytes, Urinalysis), Drug Levels, Annual Screening (Metabolic, Viral, Cancer Surveillance) |

## **C. Viral & Cytomegalovirus (CMV) Surveillance**

* **CMV Surveillance & Prophylaxis:**  
  * High-risk (Donor+/Recipient-) and standard-risk patients receive Valganciclovir prophylaxis starting 1 week post-transplant through 2–3 months.  
  * Routine CMV QNAT / PCR monitoring is conducted at 1 week, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, and 6 months post-transplant, or upon development of clinical symptoms (fever, leukopenia, graft dysfunction).  
* **COVID-19 / Respiratory Surveillance:** Monthly RT-PCR surveillance testing during the first 3 months post-transplant or immediately upon exposure/symptoms.
